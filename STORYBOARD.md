# Storyboard: Who powers France?

**Story**: electricity is produced and consumed locally. A few French regions power the others, and a green mix does not mean self-sufficiency.

**Audience**: citizens, students and regional decision makers.

**Questions**

1. Which regions produce more electricity than they consume?
2. How green is each regional mix, and how did it change?
3. What would change if a region built more solar or wind?

## Flow

| Step | Message | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | France exports low-carbon power, but regions are very different. | Text, questions |
| Key numbers | Where the selected regions stand, and the change over time. | 4 KPIs |
| Overview | The mix changes over time; nuclear dominates, renewables grow. | Line chart, stacked bars |
| Compare regions | Only a few regions produce more than they consume. | Map, ranking, scatter, small multiples |
| What if | The same increase has very different effects by region. | Sliders, scenario bars |
| Data quality | The data is reliable for monthly comparisons; limits are stated. | Checks table, heatmap |
| Insights | Cleaner mix; a few regions power the others; green share is not autonomy. | 3 takeaways, next steps |

Sidebar filters (regions, period, indicator) update the KPIs, the charts and the scenario.
